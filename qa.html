Highguard Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

What is this fandom about?

Highuard is an FPS title featuring a new IP and a unique take on the saturated hero shooter genre. The fandom contains players captivated by its gameplay as well as art direction.

Why did you choose this fandom?

I think the game is underappreciated and really fun, its developers previously worked on Apex Legends, a good game but one that got tiring over time, which is where Highguard's fresh take on the formula captivated me.

Where should new fans start?

By downloading the game! It's free and has a tutorial which does a good job of explaining what you'll really be doing in the game.
